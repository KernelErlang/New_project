# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — the first night

- The nightly ritual: draw five tarot cards, keep three (Crown, Heart, Shadow) and burn two.
  The hand is fixed for each day and unique to each Codex.
- A rules engine: aspects, palettes, musical modes, resonant numbers, awakened, banished and
  forbidden sigils, constraints, and an arcanum for each of the twenty-two Major Arcana.
- The Arcane Calculus: 29 hidden rites, discovered by experiment and recorded in the Tome.
- 30 animations and 39 sounds synthesised live with the Web Audio API.
- A 78-card procedural SVG deck, twelve heraldic Houses and a crest forge.
- A draggable parchment board, a Quill with live edict meters and an omen candle, reading
  aloud, an archive of nights, and JSON/Markdown export.
- Private by design: localhost only, a per-launch token, Host and Origin checks, a strict CSP
  and bundled fonts.
- One-click launchers for Windows, macOS and Linux. No dependencies beyond Python 3.9+.
- 300+ tests, ruff lint and format, and CI on Linux, Windows and macOS.
